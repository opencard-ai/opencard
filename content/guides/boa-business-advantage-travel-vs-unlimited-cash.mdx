# BofA Business Advantage Travel vs Unlimited Cash

If you search **Bank of America WorldPoints Travel Rewards for Business**, what you usually want is the [Business Advantage Travel Rewards World Mastercard](/en/cards/boa-biz-advantage-travel). Bank of America still uses "WorldPoints" in older marketing and third-party writeups, but the live product name is Business Advantage Travel Rewards.

Its cash sibling is the [Business Advantage Unlimited Cash Rewards Mastercard](/en/cards/boa-biz-advantage-unlimited-cash). Both have **no annual fee**. The choice is whether you want travel points or statement credits — and whether you need a card that does not charge foreign transaction fees.

People also land on this page from searches for "WorldPoints travel rewards for business visa." That query still maps to the Travel Rewards Mastercard in our data, not to a separate Visa product.

Facts below come from Bank of America's business card pages and our card records (checked October 26, 2026). Welcome offers change often, so amounts aren't quoted here — check the issuer page before you apply.

## Side by side

| | Travel Rewards (WorldPoints) | Unlimited Cash Rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | $0 | $0 |
| Everyday rate | 1.5 points per $1 on all purchases | 1.5% cash back on all purchases |
| Bonus category | 3x points on travel booked through the Bank of America Travel Center | — |
| Preferred Rewards for Business | Can raise the effective earn rate | Can raise cash back to about 1.87%–2.62% at higher tiers |
| Foreign transaction fee | None (Bank of America lists no international transaction fees) | 3% |
| How rewards pay out | Points toward travel or statement credits through BofA | Automatic cash back |

On everyday spending with no Preferred Rewards boost, the two cards earn at the same 1.5 headline rate. One pays in points; the other pays in cash.

## What "WorldPoints" actually is

WorldPoints is Bank of America's older rewards brand. On the business Travel Rewards card, you earn **1.5 points per dollar** everywhere Mastercard is accepted, and **3 points per dollar** when you book through the Bank of America Travel Center.

That 3x only helps if you actually book there. Portal inventory and prices vary, so treat 3x as a bonus for trips you were going to book in the Travel Center anyway — not as a reason to force every flight through it. Our [cash back vs. travel points guide](/en/guides/cash-back-vs-travel-points) covers how to compare a point to a dollar of cash.

## Unlimited Cash: simpler math

Unlimited Cash Rewards is the flat **1.5% cash back** card with no annual fee. If you hold a qualifying Bank of America Business checking relationship, Preferred Rewards for Business can raise that rate (Bank of America's page lists about **1.87%–2.62%** depending on tier). There is no Travel Center bonus category to manage.

The tradeoff is the **3% foreign transaction fee**. The Travel Rewards card does not charge one. If your business pays overseas suppliers or you travel for work, that alone can decide the match.

## Who should pick which

**Choose Travel Rewards (WorldPoints) if:**
- You already bank with Bank of America for business and want a no-fee travel earner
- You book some trips in the BofA Travel Center
- You need a card **without** foreign transaction fees
- You prefer points you can put toward travel rather than cash

**Choose Unlimited Cash if:**
- You want statement credits with no portal
- Preferred Rewards for Business will boost your rate
- Almost all of your spend is domestic
- You already have a separate no-FX card for overseas charges

**Skip both if:**
- You need category bonuses (office supplies, gas, ads) — look at cards like [Chase Ink Business Cash](/en/cards/chase-ink-biz-cash) instead
- You want transferable points with airline and hotel partners — [Amex Blue Business Plus](/en/cards/amex-blue-biz-plus) earns Membership Rewards instead of BofA points

## A simple year of spend

Suppose a service business puts **$40,000** a year on one card, all domestic, and never uses the Travel Center:

| Card | Rough year-one rewards (rates only) |
|---|---|
| Travel Rewards | 60,000 points (1.5 × $40,000) |
| Unlimited Cash | $600 cash (1.5% × $40,000) |

If those points are worth about a cent each when you redeem for travel or a statement credit, the two cards land in the same neighborhood. Travel Rewards pulls ahead when you book in the Travel Center at 3x, or when you avoid a foreign fee that Unlimited Cash would have charged. Unlimited Cash pulls ahead when Preferred Rewards for Business lifts the cash rate and you never touch the Travel Center.

Now add **$8,000** of overseas supplier payments. Unlimited Cash's 3% foreign fee is about **$240**. Travel Rewards charges no foreign fee on Bank of America's page. That $240 is larger than the difference between 1.5% and a slightly boosted cash rate for many small firms.

## Preferred Rewards for Business, briefly

Bank of America ties higher earn rates to deposit and investment balances in Preferred Rewards for Business. Unlimited Cash's page lists boosted cash back around **1.87%–2.62%** by tier. Travel Rewards can also benefit, but the headline story for that card is still 1.5 everywhere and 3x in the Travel Center.

If you already keep business balances at BofA, run the boosted Unlimited Cash number against Travel Rewards before you apply. If you don't, ignore the boost in your comparison — it isn't free.

## Employee cards and bookkeeping

Both products allow additional cards for employees. Rewards typically accrue to the business account. For bookkeeping, Unlimited Cash is easier to explain on a P&L ("1.5% cash back"). Travel Rewards needs a short note that points are not the same as cash until redeemed.

If several people travel, the Travel Center 3x only applies when those bookings go through BofA's portal. Company travel policies that force a different agency remove that advantage.

## Quick checklist

1. Confirm the product name on Bank of America's site — "WorldPoints" in a search usually means Business Advantage Travel Rewards.
2. Check whether you use (or would use) the BofA Travel Center before counting on 3x.
3. If you spend abroad, prefer Travel Rewards or another no-FX card over Unlimited Cash.
4. If you have Preferred Rewards for Business, run the boosted Unlimited Cash rate against Travel Rewards points.
5. Pay the statement balance in full; neither card is built for carrying a balance.

Card terms, rates, and offers change. Confirm current terms with Bank of America before applying.
