# Bank of America Business Travel Rewards vs. Unlimited Cash

If you search **Bank of America WorldPoints Travel Rewards for Business**, what you usually want is the [Business Advantage Travel Rewards World Mastercard](/en/cards/boa-biz-advantage-travel). Older materials and third-party writeups still use the "WorldPoints" name, but the current page calls the card "Business Advantage Travel Rewards World Mastercard" and does not use the WorldPoints name.

Its cash sibling is the [Business Advantage Unlimited Cash Rewards Mastercard](/en/cards/boa-biz-advantage-unlimited-cash). Both have **no annual fee**. The choice starts with travel points versus cash rewards. For overseas spending, Travel Rewards confirms no international transaction fees; check Unlimited Cash's pricing terms before comparing fees.

People also land on this page from searches for "WorldPoints travel rewards for business visa." That query still maps to the Travel Rewards Mastercard in our data, not to a separate Visa product.

Facts below come from Bank of America's business card pages and our card records (checked November 23, 2026). Welcome offers change often, so amounts aren't quoted here — check the issuer page before you apply.

## Bank of America business travel rewards: the essentials

The [Business Advantage Travel Rewards World Mastercard](/en/cards/boa-biz-advantage-travel) is a business card with no annual fee and no international transaction fees. It earns 1.5 points per $1 on everyday purchases and 3 points per $1 on travel purchases made at BofA Travel. Only travel booked at BofA Travel earns 3 points per $1.

You can redeem points for statement credits to offset travel and dining purchases, or for travel with no blackout dates at BofA Travel. This makes the redemption step worth checking: if you want cash deposited into a bank account instead, compare Unlimited Cash below. Preferred Rewards for Business can raise either card's earnings if you qualify; use the tier table rather than assuming the highest advertised rate applies to you.

## Side by side

| Annual fee | $0 | $0 |
| Everyday rate | 1.5 points per $1 on all purchases | 1.5% cash back on all purchases |

| Preferred Rewards for Business | Gold: 1.87 / 3.75; Platinum: 2.25 / 4.5; Platinum Honors: 2.62 / 5.25 points per $1 (everyday / BofA Travel) | Gold: 1.87%; Platinum: 2.25%; Platinum Honors: 2.62% |

| How rewards pay out | Statement credits to offset travel and dining; travel with no blackout dates at BofA Travel | Deposit into eligible BofA checking or savings, statement credit, or mailed check |

On everyday spending with no Preferred Rewards boost, the two cards earn at the same 1.5 headline rate. One pays in points; the other pays in cash.

## What is BofA WorldPoints (and is it the same as Business Advantage Travel Rewards)?

WorldPoints is Bank of America's older rewards brand. Here, it refers to the Business Advantage Travel Rewards card described above. On the business Travel Rewards card, you earn **1.5 points per dollar** everywhere Mastercard is accepted, and **3 points per dollar** when you book through BofA Travel.

Points can be redeemed for a statement credit to offset travel and dining purchases, or for travel with no blackout dates at BofA Travel.

Those 3 points per $1 only help if you actually book there. Portal inventory and prices vary, so treat that rate as a bonus for trips you were going to book in BofA Travel anyway — not as a reason to force every flight through it. Our [cash back vs. travel points guide](/en/guides/cash-back-vs-travel-points) covers how to compare a point to a dollar of cash.

## Unlimited Cash: simpler math

Unlimited Cash Rewards is the flat **1.5% cash back** card with no annual fee and no annual cap. Rewards do not expire as long as the account is open. Redeem as a deposit into an eligible BofA checking or savings account, as a statement credit, or as a mailed check. Preferred Rewards for Business raises the rate to **1.87% (Gold), 2.25% (Platinum) or 2.62% (Platinum Honors)**. There is no BofA Travel bonus category to manage.

For overseas spending, the Travel Rewards page explicitly lists no international transaction fees. We could not confirm Unlimited Cash's foreign transaction fee on its current product page; check its pricing terms before using it abroad.

## Who should pick which

**Choose Travel Rewards (WorldPoints) if:**

- You already bank with Bank of America for business and want a no-fee travel earner
- You book some trips in BofA Travel
- You need a card **without** foreign transaction fees
- You prefer points you can put toward travel rather than cash

**Choose Unlimited Cash if:**

- You want statement credits with no portal
- You want cash rather than points (Preferred Rewards for Business boosts both cards by the same percentage)
- Almost all of your spend is domestic
- You will check Unlimited Cash's current pricing terms before charging overseas

**Skip both if:**

- You need category bonuses (office supplies, gas, ads) — look at cards like [Chase Ink Business Cash](/en/cards/chase-ink-biz-cash) instead
- You want transferable points with airline and hotel partners — [Amex Blue Business Plus](/en/cards/amex-blue-biz-plus) earns Membership Rewards instead of BofA points

## A simple year of spend

Suppose a service business puts **$40,000** a year on one card, all domestic, and never uses BofA Travel:

| Preferred Rewards for Business tier | Travel Rewards points | Unlimited Cash rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Not enrolled | 60,000 | $600 |
| Gold | 74,800 | $748 |
| Platinum | 90,000 | $900 |
| Platinum Honors | 104,800 | $1,048 |

These calculations use the listed everyday rates: Gold earns 1.87 × 40,000 = 74,800 points or 1.87% × $40,000 = $748; Platinum Honors earns 2.62 × 40,000 = 104,800 points or 2.62% × $40,000 = $1,048. They exclude welcome offers and BofA Travel bookings.

If those points are worth about a cent each when redeemed for eligible travel or dining statement credits, the two cards land in the same neighborhood at the same tier. Travel Rewards offers extra points on BofA Travel bookings and a confirmed no-international-transaction-fee benefit. Preferred Rewards for Business boosts both cards, so compare both at your qualifying tier rather than boosting only Unlimited Cash.

For overseas supplier payments, Travel Rewards has a confirmed no-international-transaction-fee benefit. Without a verified Unlimited Cash fee, we do not calculate a dollar fee difference between the two cards.

## Preferred Rewards for Business: tiers and qualifying balances

Both cards receive the following Preferred Rewards for Business bonuses. Travel Rewards rates are points per $1; the two rates in its column are everyday purchases / travel purchases through BofA Travel.

| Tier | Bonus | Travel Rewards everyday / BofA Travel | Unlimited Cash |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not enrolled | 0% | 1.5 / 3 points | 1.5% |
| Gold | 25% | 1.87 / 3.75 points | 1.87% |
| Platinum | 50% | 2.25 / 4.5 points | 2.25% |
| Platinum Honors | 75% | 2.62 / 5.25 points | 2.62% |

Enrollment has no fee. To qualify, you need a Bank of America business checking account and a three-month combined daily balance in qualifying BofA business deposit and/or Merrill business investment accounts of at least **$20,000 for Gold, $50,000 for Platinum or $100,000 for Platinum Honors**.

If you already keep qualifying business balances at BofA or Merrill, compare both cards at your tier before applying. Otherwise, use the unenrolled rates unless you plan to meet the balance requirements; any cost of moving or maintaining balances belongs in your comparison.

## Does BofA say what credit limit you'll get?

The product pages do not list a credit limit or a minimum. Your credit limit is set when you are approved.

## Employee cards and bookkeeping

Both products allow additional cards for employees. Rewards typically accrue to the business account. For bookkeeping, Unlimited Cash is easier to explain on a P&L ("1.5% cash back"). Travel Rewards needs a short note that points are not the same as cash until redeemed.

If several people travel, BofA Travel's 3 points per $1 only apply when those bookings go through BofA's portal. Company travel policies that force a different agency remove that advantage.

## Quick checklist

1. Confirm the product name on Bank of America's site — "WorldPoints" in a search usually means Business Advantage Travel Rewards.
2. Check whether you use (or would use) BofA Travel before counting on 3 points per $1.
3. If you spend abroad, verify pricing terms; Travel Rewards explicitly lists no international transaction fees.
4. If you have Preferred Rewards for Business, compare both cards at your tier; the boost applies to each.
5. Pay the statement balance in full; neither card is built for carrying a balance.

Card terms, rates, and offers change. Confirm current terms with Bank of America before applying.
